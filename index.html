What is recharge software and how does it work?+

Recharge software lets businesses offer supported recharges and related services through a portal or app, with tools to track wallets and transactions.

Can I integrate the recharge API into my website or app?+

API access can connect supported recharge services to your own website, application or business platform. Ask the team for integration details.

Can I launch the platform under my own brand?+

White-label options may include your brand identity across supported website, app and admin experiences. Features depend on the chosen plan.

How do commissions work?+

Commission depends on operator, service and plan. Request the current rate card before choosing a plan.
